# Interview prep: Senior Frontend Engineer (conversational UI)

Profile: example (fictional CV)

## Process and format
- Not stated in the vacancy. Ask: number of stages, who interviews, length of each.

## Likely questions
- ❌ "8+ years of professional software engineering experience" – they will probe the gap.
  CV: "Frontend engineer, 6 years, mostly Angular and TypeScript". Frame: say six years plainly, then move to scope (data-heavy dashboards, real-time).
- ❓ "lead complex initiatives" – not in the CV. Ask the user first whether they led one end to end; if yes, mark it `confirmed by you`.
- ✅ "Experience building real-time, asynchronous, event-driven user experiences" – CV: "Added real-time updates over WebSocket". Frame: open with the WebSocket work; add scope only if you can state it.
- ⚠️ "Mentor and coach engineers" – CV: "Reviewed code and mentored two junior developers". Frame: two juniors, through review; don't call it a lead role.

## Weak spots
- Years: six against eight-plus. Say it directly, then point to the closest-matching work.
- Initiative leadership: answer only from what you confirmed.

## Brush up
- Streaming responses and typing states in the UI (the vacancy's core problem).
- React basics, since the vacancy says "TypeScript and React or Angular" – the CV is Angular.

## Money and format
- Not stated in the vacancy. Give your range from the profile; ask early whether a non-UK contractor is possible.

## Your questions for them
- Can this be done from outside the UK, and as what: employee or contractor? Decides if it is possible at all.
- Who owns frontend architecture decisions today? Shows what would be yours to decide.

## Before the call
- CV open, two episodes ready (WebSocket, mentoring), questions written down, link and time zone checked.
