# Questions bank

Pick only what the vacancy did not answer. Add 2–3 specific to this vacancy.
Each question gets a one-line reason.

## Role and scope

- What does a normal week look like – how much is new features, how much support, bugs, tech debt?
- Who makes the key decisions today (architecture, strategy, scope), and how? What would be mine to decide?
- What did the last person in this role (or the team) ship in the last 3 months?
- Which tools, systems or methods does the team use now, and is any migration or upgrade in progress or planned?
- How many people work in my specialty here, and who reviews my work?

## Growth

- What would success look like in 6 and 12 months?
- Is there a path from this role to a lead or architect one? Can you give an example of someone who went through it?
- Is there time for learning, tech debt or experiments, and how is it planned?
- Is there a budget or practice for conferences, courses, internal talks?
- Who would I learn from on this team?
- How do you give feedback – how often, and in what form?

## Team and process

- How is work planned and estimated? Who talks to the client or product?
- What does the release process look like? How often do you deploy?
- What testing do you have – unit, integration, e2e? Who owns quality?
- How do you handle a blocked task or a changed requirement mid-sprint?
- Is the team in one timezone? What overlap hours are expected?

## Product and company

- What is the product, who uses it, and where is it heading in the next year?
- Is the project funded long-term? What happens to the team if the contract ends? (staff augmentation)
- Why is this position open – growth or replacement?
- What was the hardest technical problem the team solved recently?

## Money and contract

- Is the range gross or net? What currency?
- Contract type: employee, FOP, contractor? Who pays for equipment, vacation, sick days?
- Is part-time possible, and what are the exact hours?
- Is there a review of pay, and how often?
- Business travel: how often and who pays?

## Vacancy-specific (always add)

For each unclear or suspicious phrase in the vacancy, write one question that clears it up.
Example: the vacancy says "lead initiatives" but also "80% autonomy" – ask which one is true.
