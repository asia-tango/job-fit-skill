# job-fit: what I can do

Paste a vacancy, attach your CV, and pick a step. Click a phrase to copy it, then paste it into the chat. Nothing is sent anywhere by this page, and I never send anything for you.

## 1. Set up your profile (once)
Reads your CV, asks a short multiple-choice quiz (pay, format, where you can work, what you want and what you avoid), and gives you a `profile.md` to keep.
[[cmd: Set me up | Set up my profile from the attached CV]]
[[cmd: Start over | reset]]

## 2. Analyze one vacancy
Verdict, requirements against your CV with quoted proof, CV fixes, growth, red flags, questions for the recruiter, talking points for your application.
[[cmd: Analyze | Analyze this vacancy against my CV: <paste the text>]]

## 3. Compare several vacancies
A summary table with a verdict for each, the patterns across them, and the first thing to check on each.
[[cmd: Compare | Compare these vacancies against my CV: <paste the texts>]]

## 4. Prepare for an interview
Likely questions tied to the vacancy, the CV episode for each, honest answers for weak spots, topics to brush up, money, and your questions for them.
[[cmd: Full prep | I got an interview invite for this vacancy. Prepare me.]]
[[cmd: Weak spots only | Prepare me for the weak spots only: the gaps and the things not in my CV.]]

## 5. Get it as a file
Every result is a page you can read, print or save as PDF.
[[cmd: PDF | Give me this report as a PDF]]

---

What I won't do: add experience that is not in your CV, send anything for you, or write answers for you to read out. I give frames and facts; the words are yours.
