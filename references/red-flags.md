# Red flags

Universal flags for any role. Always quote the line that triggered the flag.
A flag is a reason to ask, not always a reason to skip.
The user's own "avoid" list (from Setup) is added on top. Never assume a personal dislike applies to someone else.

## Location and legal

- "EU only", "UK based", "must have the right to work in…", visa not mentioned for an international hire.
- Office days or travel that don't fit the user's constraints. Ask how often and who pays.
- Employee-only when the user works as a contractor or sole proprietor (or the reverse).

## Title vs scope

- Junior or middle title with senior duties, or senior title with ticket-only duties.
- Years asked far above the scope offered, or the reverse.
- "Lead" or "Head" with no mention of people, budget, decisions or strategy.
- A required certificate or licence the CV doesn't have. That is a real gap: editing the CV won't fix it.

## Money

- Salary missing and the recruiter avoids a range.
- Gross or net not stated; currency unclear.
- Large variable part ("bonus up to…") on a low base.
- A number far below market for the scope. Check a salary source before saying so; say where the figure comes from.
- Job-board "estimated salary" is not the company's number.

## Culture words with no facts

- "Fast-paced", "intense", "we are a family", "wear many hats", "rockstar", "self-starter".
- Passion words repeated more than the process is described.

## Process smells

- Take-home task that needs more than a few hours, or unpaid work that looks like real work.
- Many rounds with no feedback between them.
- A vacancy copied from a template: a long list of unrelated tools or skills as Must.
- "Ownership" with no authority: responsible for results, no decision rights.
- Application instructions that look like a trap ("start your message with…") – fine to follow, but notice what it filters for.

## Stability

- Staff augmentation or outsourcing where the end client can stop the project; ask the typical length.
- A new team or "startup phase" with no information about funding.
- Why the seat is open is unknown. Ask.

## Mismatch hidden behind the title or tags

- The title or tag names your field, but the requirement text describes a different job. Read the requirements, not the tags.
- Wide role ("full-stack", "generalist", "everything") where the part you want is a small share.
- Required tools that signal a different specialty from your CV. Check the user's `stack` and `avoid` lists.
